The honest problem

Everyone knows scenarios work. Almost nobody builds them.

Ask a learner "what would you do?" instead of "what do you know?" and the lesson sticks in a way slides never manage. You already know this. It's not the part anyone argues with.

The part that stops people is the build. Branching scenarios mean mapping every decision path, writing a believable consequence for each wrong turn, and forcing messy choices into a gradebook. So good intentions quietly become another quiz.

This course is about the way past that — not a better maze, no maze at all.

The course
